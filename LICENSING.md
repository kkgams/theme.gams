historical The98 audit and maintenance records. Its `theme.the98` identity
describes that audit's original scope, not the current collection's repository
name. Removing historical comparison data from the archive is not a new legal
audit or a change to license terms.

Release checks require nonempty regular LICENSE/NOTICE files, exact legal
payloads, NOTICE's evidence binding, every recorded input digest and the complete
source closure. Historical evidence and required attribution are not replaced
by a version bump. New or changed theme sources need corresponding reviewed
source/license evidence before release checks can pass.

licenses belong to their own distributions.
